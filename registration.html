ICIFMEDE 2027

Registration Guidelines

Instructions for Registration

  • Participants are requested to carefully read the conference information and registration guidelines before completing the registration form.
  • Please enter your full name, email address, mobile number and institutional details accurately.
  • Authors should provide the paper title exactly as submitted for the conference.
  • Please select the appropriate conference track based on the subject area of your research.
  • All mandatory fields must be completed before submitting the registration form.
  • Authors are advised to complete registration after receiving the paper acceptance notification, wherever applicable.
  • Registration fee details and payment instructions will be provided separately by the conference organizing committee.
  • Participants are requested to retain their registration confirmation and payment details for future reference.

Important Note

Registration and paper acceptance are separate processes. Submission of a registration form does not guarantee paper acceptance. Paper acceptance will be based on the conference review process and the decision of the organizing committee.
