ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

ICIFMEDE 2027

2nd International Conference on Innovations and Future Scope of Mechanical Engineering in Digital Era

The 2nd International Conference on Innovations and Future Scope of Mechanical Engineering in Digital Era (ICIFMEDE 2027) will be held on 10th & 11th June 2027 at Anurag University, Hyderabad.

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals and experts from different disciplines to share their latest research, innovations, technologies and ideas in the field of Mechanical Engineering and its emerging interdisciplinary applications.

The conference provides a common platform for knowledge sharing, technical discussions, research collaboration and exploring the future scope of Mechanical Engineering in the digital era.

Conference Date

10 - 11 June 2027

Venue

Anurag University, Hyderabad

Conference

ICIFMEDE 2027

Conference Objectives

Knowledge Sharing

To provide a platform for researchers, academicians and industry professionals to share recent developments and innovative ideas.

Innovation

To promote innovative solutions and emerging technologies in Mechanical Engineering and related disciplines.

Collaboration

To encourage research collaboration between academic institutions, industries and interdisciplinary research communities.

Scope of the Conference

The conference covers a broad spectrum of Mechanical Engineering and interdisciplinary areas including advanced manufacturing, additive manufacturing, robotics and automation, artificial intelligence, digital engineering, energy systems, materials, thermal sciences, design engineering and emerging technologies.

Interdisciplinary Focus

ICIFMEDE 2027 encourages contributions that connect Mechanical Engineering with Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Electronics, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, Agriculture, Energy and other emerging areas.

Expected Outcomes

The conference is expected to facilitate knowledge exchange, research networking, technology development and new opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration among participants from academia, research organizations and industry.
