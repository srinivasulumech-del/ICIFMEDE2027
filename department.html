Department of Mechanical Engineering

Department of Mechanical Engineering was established in the year 2004 and was accredited by NBA under tier-I in 2015. It was reaccredited subsequently twice with validity extending up to June 2025. The department comprises of well-qualified and committed faculty members and technical staff, among which many are doctorates with diverse specialisations.
