CALL FOR PAPERS

ICIFMEDE 2027

Call for Papers

Authors are invited to contribute to the 2nd International Conference on Innovations and Future Scope of Mechanical Engineering in Digital Era (ICIFMEDE 2027) by submitting high-quality, original and unpublished research papers, review articles and industrial experiences.

The conference provides an excellent platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, research scholars and students to present their latest findings, exchange knowledge, discuss emerging technologies and establish meaningful research collaborations.

Who Can Submit?

Contributions are welcome from individuals and organizations working in Mechanical Engineering and related interdisciplinary areas. The conference encourages participation from both academia and industry.

Faculty & Academicians

Faculty members and academicians working in engineering, science and interdisciplinary research areas.

Research Scholars

Ph.D. scholars and research researchers presenting innovative research and emerging technologies.

Industry Professionals

Industry professionals and R&D teams sharing industrial experiences, applications and technological developments.

Students

UG and PG students with original research, innovative projects and emerging engineering applications.

Paper Submission

Authors are encouraged to submit original and unpublished manuscripts addressing significant research problems, technological developments, innovative methodologies or industrial applications related to the conference themes.

03

Peer Review

Submitted papers will undergo an appropriate review and evaluation process.

04

Presentation

Accepted contributions will be considered for presentation at the conference.

Submission Guidelines

  • Submitted work should be original and unpublished.
  • The manuscript should clearly present the research objectives, methodology, results and conclusions.
  • Papers should be relevant to one or more of the conference tracks.
  • Authors should follow the prescribed paper format and submission guidelines.
  • Authors are responsible for ensuring the originality and accuracy of their submitted work.
  • Further details regarding paper format and submission procedure will be announced on the conference website.

Review & Selection

All submitted manuscripts will be evaluated based on their originality, technical quality, relevance to the conference themes, clarity of presentation and potential contribution to the field. The review process is intended to ensure that high-quality research and innovative contributions are presented at ICIFMEDE 2027.

Research Presentation & Knowledge Exchange

ICIFMEDE 2027 aims to provide an engaging environment for researchers and professionals to present their work, receive valuable feedback and interact with experts from diverse engineering and interdisciplinary domains. The conference will encourage technical discussions, knowledge sharing and opportunities for future research collaboration.
